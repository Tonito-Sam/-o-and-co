# O&Co WorkspaceOS — Client-Ready Deliverables Note

## Overview

Following the presentation of the WorkspaceOS mockup, the next step is to move from a polished prototype into a more realistic, operational experience for O&Co.

To support this, we recommend that O&Co provide a small set of core content, asset, and data inputs that will make the platform feel authentic and ready for onboarding, tenant engagement, and internal use.

## What we are expecting from O&Co

### 1. Core content for the welcome and onboarding experience
O&Co should provide:
- welcome page copy and key messaging,
- a short overview of the workspace experience,
- value proposition language for members, tenants, and vendors,
- and any calls to action they would like featured.

### 2. Photography and visual assets
To make the experience feel real, O&Co should provide or confirm:
- branch and workspace photography,
- images of meeting rooms, lounges, cafés, and community spaces,
- team or tenant lifestyle imagery,
- and supporting visuals for marketplace listings and promotional content.

### 3. Profile and operational data
The platform will be much more useful if O&Co provides:
- tenant and company names,
- tenant admin contacts,
- branch associations,
- office or membership details,
- billing contacts,
- and any basic operational information needed for day-to-day use.

### 4. Marketplace and community content
For the marketplace, community feed, and content hub, O&Co can provide:
- vendor and listing information,
- product or service descriptions,
- promo and banner content,
- event announcements,
- and podcast or community content that can be uploaded directly or shared via links once produced.

## Content ownership approach

We recommend a phased approach:
- O&Co provides the initial core content needed to make the experience feel complete.
- The platform includes editable fields so that future updates can be managed directly.
- Over time, tenants and vendors can self-manage their own profiles and listings where appropriate.

## Notes on brand and visual direction

The current visual direction is already working well and aligns with the experience we presented. For now, additional formal brand development is not required unless O&Co wishes to formalise the system further at a later stage.

## Recommended next steps

1. Share the welcome page copy and positioning text.
2. Provide any available photography or brand assets.
3. Share a starter set of tenant, vendor, and branch information.
4. Confirm how community and podcast content will be published going forward.
5. Decide which profile and listing updates should be managed by O&Co directly versus by users themselves.

This will allow the prototype to evolve from a strong visual mockup into a more realistic and usable operating platform for O&Co.
