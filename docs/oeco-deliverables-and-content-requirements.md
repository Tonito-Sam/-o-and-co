# O&Co WorkspaceOS — Deliverables and Content Requirements

## 1. Purpose

This document outlines the deliverables required to move the O&Co WorkspaceOS prototype from a polished mockup into a realistic, launch-ready experience.

It captures:
- the core user types and their needs,
- the content and assets required from O&Co,
- the expected data inputs for the platform,
- and the recommended approach for content ownership and future self-service updates.

---

## 2. Project Context

The current build is a working UI prototype for a multi-role workspace platform covering:
- member and tenant experience,
- branch and facility operations,
- community engagement,
- marketplace and café commerce,
- billing and finance workflows,
- content management,
- and role-based access for internal and external users.

The next phase is to turn this into a realistic operating system that O&Co can use for onboarding, tenant engagement, and internal operations.

---

## 3. Deliverables Required from O&Co

### A. Brand and Visual Assets
O&Co should provide the following to make the experience feel authentic and launch-ready:

- official brand guidelines if they want to formalise the system later
- logo files in usable formats (PNG/SVG, transparent where needed)
- photography of:
  - branches and workspaces,
  - reception areas,
  - meeting rooms and boardrooms,
  - café spaces,
  - community areas,
  - events and networking spaces,
  - team members and tenant representatives
- lifestyle and lifestyle-led imagery for the welcome page and community content
- images for marketplace listings, vendors, and promo banners

### B. Welcome Page Content
The welcome page should be populated with content that reflects O&Co’s actual identity and positioning.

O&Co should provide:
- a short hero headline and supporting copy
- a clear value proposition for members, tenants, and vendors
- a short company overview for the About section
- key benefits of the workspace experience
- testimonials or quotes if available
- a clear call to action for new users

### C. Profile and Tenant Data
The platform needs realistic profile data for users and organisations.

Required data includes:
- tenant / company names
- contact persons for each tenant
- tenant admin names and email addresses
- branch association for each tenant
- office size, desk count, or membership type
- billing contact details
- preferred communication channels
- profile photo or company logo if available

### D. Marketplace Content
If the marketplace is to feel real and usable, O&Co should provide or confirm:
- vendor names and profiles
- product and service categories
- listing titles and descriptions
- pricing and promo information
- product images and gallery images
- service banners or featured visuals
- vendor onboarding details
- payment and invoicing preferences

### E. Community and Content Hub Content
To support the community and content modules, O&Co should provide:
- event titles and descriptions
- upcoming community activities
- announcement copy
- short news or editorial content
- media assets for content cards
- links to videos, podcasts, or articles if available
- podcast or community content that can be attached as uploads or shared via links once produced

### F. Operational Data
To make the experience useful for daily operations, O&Co should also provide:
- branch list and branch descriptions
- availability and capacity information for rooms/spaces
- facility and booking rules
- billing terms and invoice handling preferences
- support or service escalation structure

## 5. Recommended Content Ownership Model

For the first version, the recommended approach is a hybrid model:

### Option 1 — O&Co provides the baseline content
This is best for the initial launch and demo readiness.
- O&Co supplies the foundational brand, photos, and core copy.
- The platform shows a polished and credible experience from day one.

### Option 2 — Users self-manage their own profiles later
Once the system is live, certain user types can update their own content.
- tenants update their own profiles and company details,
- vendors update listings and images,
- admins update branch and operational content.

### Recommended approach
Use a phased model:
1. O&Co provides the core content and assets for launch.
2. The platform includes editable fields for future self-service updates.
3. Over time, tenants and vendors can manage their own profile and listing content.

This keeps the initial rollout efficient while still allowing scalability.
